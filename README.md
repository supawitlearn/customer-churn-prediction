# customer-churn-prediction
## Data-driven approaches to predicting customer churn in a non-contractual car-sharing company [📎Publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590198225002799)
The study investigates customer churn in a car-sharing business in Thailand, a topic rarely explored in the sharing economy due to challenges in tracking customer behavior across segments. Six data-driven models—with and without SMOTE data balancing—were tested. Results show that the best-performing models vary by user type: the Transformer (without SMOTE) for one-time users, ANN (without SMOTE) for frequent users, and XGBoost for infrequent users. Key churn factors differ across segments, highlighting the need for segment-specific retention strategies. Overall, stronger financial and service engagement reduces the likelihood of churn.
